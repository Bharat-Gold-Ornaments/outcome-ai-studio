# Business AI Partner

Create a polished, premium, modern one-page website for an AI consulting and automation business.

IMPORTANT:

This is NOT a generic "AI agency" website.

It should position the business as a practical AI consulting partner for businesses.

The core proposition is:

"We help businesses identify where AI can create measurable value, implement practical AI systems, and help their teams adopt them."

The business focuses specifically on three areas:

SALES

MARKETING

CUSTOMER SERVICE

These are the primary areas of expertise and should be visually prominent throughout the website.

The three core services are:

1. AI CONSULTING

2. AI AUTOMATION

3. AI TRAINING

There is also an ongoing service:

4. AI PARTNERSHIP / RETAINER

The commercial journey should be communicated clearly:

FREE DISCOVERY CALL

↓

AI CONSULTING / BUSINESS AI AUDIT

↓

IDENTIFY OPPORTUNITIES

↓

PRIORITISE USE CASES

↓

AI ROADMAP

↓

AI AUTOMATION

↓

IMPLEMENTATION

↓

AI TRAINING

↓

ONGOING AI PARTNERSHIP

Do not present these as disconnected services. They are stages of one AI transformation journey.

DESIGN DIRECTION

==================================================

Create a premium, sophisticated B2B website.

The visual language should sit somewhere between:

- premium consulting firm

- modern SaaS company

- technology consultancy

- editorial business publication

It should NOT look like:

- generic AI agency

- crypto website

- cyberpunk website

- futuristic robot website

- cheap startup template

- overly colourful SaaS landing page

COLOUR PALETTE:

Primary:

Warm off-white / ivory background.

Text:

Near-black / charcoal.

Secondary:

Soft grey.

Accent:

One restrained sophisticated accent colour.

Do not use many accent colours.

TYPOGRAPHY:

Use a premium modern sans-serif.

Large, confident headlines.

Excellent typography hierarchy.

Generous whitespace.

Strong readability.

LAYOUT:

Use generous margins.

Avoid excessive cards everywhere.

Use a mixture of:

- editorial sections

- large typography

- process diagrams

- subtle cards

- timelines

- whitespace

Make the three focus areas — Sales, Marketing and Customer Service — visually prominent.

==================================================

VISUAL STYLE

==================================================

Use abstract diagrams, workflow visualisations and subtle interface-inspired graphics.

Prefer showing:

processes

systems

flows

connections

business workflows

rather than generic AI imagery.

No stock photos are required for the initial version.

==================================================

ANIMATION

==================================================

Use subtle animations only.

Examples:

- gentle fade-in on scroll

- process steps appearing sequentially

- subtle card hover

- button hover

- small movement in abstract workflow visuals

Do NOT use:

- excessive parallax

- flashy transitions

- constant floating objects

- distracting animations

The website should feel fast.
